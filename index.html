About Me

Since I was a teen, I have always had a love and passion for music. I felt and still feel it was in God’s plans for me to use my gift of music to lead people to christ. As a result, I have been writing original songs for years. Unlike most artist, my songs are not written to typically fit a certain single genre. My songs focus more on the lyrics instead. Because of this, I like to think my songs fit multiple different genres depending on which genre fits best. With my dad being a music minister, growing up I have had multiple chances to perform my original songs as well as songs by other artist in front of an audience. You can say that after years of doing this, I have become very comfortable being on a stage performing in front of thousands of people. Every time I perform, I feel like I am exactly where I belong.
